## What can an Ontario landlord do when a tenant stops paying rent?

Suggested URL: `/guides/ontario-tenant-stops-paying-rent`

If an Ontario tenant misses rent, first verify the amount owed and contact the tenant in writing. If the tenancy is covered by Ontario’s Residential Tenancies Act, you can use the Landlord and Tenant Board’s non-payment process. An N4 is a notice, not an eviction order. Do not change the locks or remove the tenant yourself.

1. **Update the rent ledger.** Record the rent due, its due date, payments and credits. Keep copies of the lease, receipts and messages. If a $2,500 payment is due and $1,000 has been credited, verify that the unpaid rent for that period is $1,500 before deciding what to put on any form. Do not assume other fees count as rent.
2. **Decide what you are trying to resolve.** If you and the tenant reach a payment arrangement, put the amounts and dates in writing. If you plan to seek eviction for non-payment, read the current N4 instructions on the LTB site before serving the notice. Check the termination date and service rules that apply to your case.
3. **Check whether you can apply.** If the tenant is still in the unit and the N4 has not been voided, read the L1 instructions for when and how to apply. If you only want an order for payment, or the tenant has moved out, the LTB explains separate forms and rules. Keep the proof of service and update the ledger if the tenant pays.

This is general information. If you are unsure which process applies, seek Ontario legal advice before serving a notice or filing.

**Sources:** [LTB: If a Tenant Does Not Pay Rent](https://tribunalsontario.ca/documents/ltb/Brochures/If%20a%20Tenant%20Does%20Not%20Pay%20Rent.html) · [LTB application and hearing process](https://tribunalsontario.ca/ltb/application-and-hearing-process/)

## Rent Guarantee vs. landlord insurance in Ontario

Suggested URL: `/guides/rent-guarantee-vs-landlord-insurance-ontario`

Ask what caused the loss. Unpaid rent because a tenant stopped paying and lost rental income because a property is uninhabitable after an insured event are different situations. Do not assume either agreement covers both. The wording, limits, exclusions and claim requirements determine what applies.

| Situation | What to check in writing |
| --- | --- |
| The tenant stays in the unit but stops paying. | Ask whether the agreement responds to non-payment and which tenancy, arrears, notice and documentation rules apply. |
| A fire makes the unit uninhabitable. | Ask your insurer whether rental income loss is included, what insured event must occur, and how long the benefit lasts. |
| Someone alleges an injury at the property. | Ask the insurer about premises liability and any exclusions. Do not rely on a rent non-payment agreement for liability protection. |

Before comparing offers, write down who issues each agreement, when it takes effect, the event that triggers a claim, and what documents you would need to submit. Ask specifically about existing arrears and how overlapping agreements would respond. This guide does not confirm any specific benefit, premium, payout timing or eligibility. QuoteUs.ca is a marketplace, not the insurer.

## Can I get car insurance with a G2 licence in Ontario?

Suggested URL: `/guides/ontario-auto-insurance-g2-licence`

Yes. A valid Ontario G2 driver can request auto insurance. If you own the vehicle, ask about a policy for that vehicle. If you drive a household member’s vehicle, tell the policyholder and have them confirm how you should be listed. A quote request does not put insurance in force, and a policy does not remove G2 licence restrictions.

- Tell the insurer your licence class and when you earned it.
- Explain which vehicle you drive, who owns it, and who uses it most. Describe actual use rather than assigning a driver just to change a quote.
- Have your driver training details, driving history and previous insurance information available if requested.
- Compare the same limits, deductibles and options across quotes. Ask when coverage would start and get confirmation before driving.
- Check Ontario’s official G2 conditions, including rules that can apply to young drivers carrying passengers at night.

For example, if you borrow a parent’s car regularly to commute, describe that schedule when asking about the household policy. If you later buy your own car, get a fresh quote using the ownership and use details for that vehicle.

**Sources:** [Ontario: Get a G driver’s licence](https://www.ontario.ca/page/get-g-drivers-licence-new-drivers) · [FSRA: Purchasing auto insurance](https://www.fsrao.ca/consumers/auto-insurance/purchasing-your-policy/purchasing-auto-insurance)

## What information do I need to get an Ontario car insurance quote?

Suggested URL: `/guides/ontario-auto-insurance-quote-checklist`

Start with the drivers, the vehicle and how it will be used. Have your licence class and driving history ready, along with the vehicle’s year, make and model, where it is parked and how far you expect to drive. A broker or insurer can tell you whether they need a VIN or any additional details for a firm quote.

| Gather | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Drivers | Licence class and licensing dates, regular drivers, relevant convictions and claims, and previous coverage information. |
| Vehicle | Year, make, model and trim; VIN if available; ownership, lease or financing details. |
| Use | Where it is primarily kept, annual distance, commute distance and any business use. |
| Policy choices | Target start date, liability limits, deductibles, and optional coverages to compare. |

**Comparison tip:** If one quote has a $500 deductible and another has a $1,000 deductible, you are not comparing identical coverage terms. Ask for both quotes with the same deductible before comparing premiums. If you have not bought the vehicle yet, ask what a preliminary quote needs and what may change when you confirm the exact vehicle.

Share licence or policy details only through the insurer’s or broker’s approved channel. A quote is not proof of insurance.
