---
name: Annual Rent Guarantee pricing
description: Annual percentage inputs are additive; monthly pricing is unchanged.
---

For Rent Guarantee → Pricing → Annual, add the entered “Total %” to the RG percentage to produce Total Deduction. The annual Total % input represents the non-RG commission portion, not an inclusive combined rate.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed they need “the sum of both together.” Do not subtract the RG rate from that annual input.

**How to apply:** Keep the annual breakdown and payment default consistent with this sum. Preserve existing stored RG and commission rates without a data rewrite. Monthly pricing remains inclusive unless separately requested.
