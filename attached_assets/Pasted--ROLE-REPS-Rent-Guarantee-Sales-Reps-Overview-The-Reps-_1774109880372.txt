🔐 ROLE: REPS (Rent Guarantee Sales Reps)
🧩 Overview

The Reps role is designed for internal sales agents who manage and generate their own Rent Guarantee leads, similar to brokers—but with additional control over lead creation and document collection.

👤 Permissions & Access

Reps can:

Access a personalized dashboard
View leads assigned to them
Create and manage their own leads/accounts
Send document request links to tenants and landlords
Track document upload status
Update lead/application progress

Reps cannot:

Access other reps' leads
Access broker-level analytics (unless granted)
Approve/underwrite applications (admin only)

Sections:

➕ Lead / Account Creation

Reps can manually create a new Rent Guarantee lead:

Required Fields:
Tenant Name
Landlord Name
Property Address
Monthly Rent Amount
Tenant Email
Tenant Phone
Employment Status
Optional Fields:
Co-applicant info
Notes
Move-in date
📩 Document Request System
Feature: “Send Document Request”

Reps can send a secure upload link via:

Email
SMS (optional)
Recipients:
Tenant
Landlord
📎 Documents That Can Be Requested:

Tenant Documents:

Government ID
Job Letter / Proof of Employment
Credit Report
Pay Stubs
Bank Statements

Landlord Documents:

Rental Agreement / Lease
Proof of Ownership
Insurance Documents
🔗 Upload Experience (User Side)

When tenant/landlord clicks the link:

No login required
Secure upload portal
Can upload multiple files
Shows checklist of required documents
Progress indicator (e.g., 3/5 documents uploaded)
📂 Document Management (Rep View)

Each lead will include:

Document checklist
Upload status:
✅ Received
⏳ Pending
Ability to:
View files
Download files
Request missing documents again

🔔 Notifications

Reps receive alerts when:

A document is uploaded
